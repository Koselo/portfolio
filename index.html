About

My name is Michal Podskoč and I am a UX/UI designer with five years of experience.

Studied marketing communication and advertising in Nitra, worked at Sygic, and today I design digital products at Slovenská sporiteľňa.

I enjoy talking to users, understanding their needs, and turning insights into clear and meaningful design. I love the moment when scattered ideas start forming a structure and a concept becomes a usable interface that actually makes sense.

What I enjoy most is creating something people can understand instantly, without explanation. I believe in clear and simple digital experiences that feel natural to use, with a small touch of humor when it fits.
