Project spotlight

Calculator Redesign & Erste Visual Standard

Together with colleagues, we worked on a project that went beyond a simple redesign. It involved rethinking the Erste visual standard while also rebuilding the user interface of several financial calculators across Slovenská sporiteľňa. It was an extensive project with many moving parts — and one that pushed the whole team forward.

This project taught me a lot personally. It showed me just how difficult it is to balance calculation accuracy with ease of use in a way that delivers real value to the client. We collaborated with specialists across the entire bank and gained a much deeper understanding of the products we were designing for.

Working closely with UX researcher colleagues was especially valuable. Seeing how thoroughly they understood our clients — and how precisely they could translate that understanding into actionable design direction at every step — was something I carry with me to this day.

Thanks also go to colleagues across the Customer Experience Design team for their open feedback and their willingness to look at challenges from many different angles.
