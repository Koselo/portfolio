Challenge

My old portfolio was functional — but it wasn't doing its job. The design felt dated, the structure was unclear, and it didn't reflect the quality of work I was actually delivering for clients.

A portfolio is a live argument for why someone should hire you. Mine wasn't making that argument convincingly. I needed a complete redesign — one that felt intentional, showed the right things, and could be built and maintained without a framework or backend.

What I Delivered

01

UX Design

Mapped out the full information architecture — what to show, in what order, and why. Defined user flows for recruiters and potential clients with different goals, and wireframed the layout before committing to visuals.

02

UI Design

Designed a clean, typographically driven visual system. Minimal palette, strong hierarchy, and enough personality to stand out — without pulling focus from the work itself.

03

Design to Code

Built the entire site from scratch in HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no build tools. Pixel-perfect implementation of the design, fully responsive, with smooth animations and a page loader.

04

Project Case Studies

Wrote and structured individual case study pages for each project — covering challenge, deliverables, process, and outcomes in a consistent, readable format.
